Bachelor's thesis

BSc Creative Computing · USTP · 2024

Learning nature survival skills in a playful VR world

Question
How can gamified VR teach basic survival skills while keeping learners engaged and safe?
Built
A Unity VR forest for Meta Quest with stations for shelter building, fire making, water purification, animal tracks and edible vs. poisonous plants. Spatial audio with Wwise.
Study
Participants aged 9 to 70, split into a VR group and a printed-guide group. Pre/post tests, surveys, interviews and observation.
Found
Both groups learned. VR was described as “very fun” and “interactive” and worked well for spatial, situational knowledge like tracks and animal encounters, while the guide did better on fine visual details like telling berries apart. Lesson: in VR, the details that matter need to be designed to stand out.
